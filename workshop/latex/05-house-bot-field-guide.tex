\tierband{mygreen}{Beginner}{Route 01 \textperiodcentered{} fixed patterns and copying}
\botrow{Rocky}{rocky} & Always plays rock. & Play paper. You should win every throw. \\[1pt]

\hairline
\botrow{Cycle RPS}{cycle\_rps} & Plays rock, paper, scissors, then repeats. & Work out the next move in the cycle and play the move that beats it. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{Bot}{RPSRPSRPSR}}
\hairline
\botrow{Copycat}{copycat} & Copies your previous throw after a random opening. & If you last played rock, it will play rock next. Use your own history to choose a reply. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{You}{RPPSRSSP}}
\demo{\seq{Bot}{QRPPSRSS}\note{one throw behind you}}
\hairline
\botrow{Echo Two}{echo\_two} & Copies your throw from two turns ago. & Use \code{my\_history[-2]} once two throws have finished. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{You}{RPSSRPRS}}
\demo{\seq{Bot}{QQRPSSRP}\note{two throws behind you}}
\tierband{myblue}{Intermediate}{Route 02 \textperiodcentered{} probabilities and drift}
\botrow{Biased Random}{biased\_random} & Independent throws: 60\% rock, 25\% paper, 15\% scissors. & Paper has expected net payoff +0.45 per throw. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{Bot}{RPRRSRPRRS}\note{a sample: about six rocks in ten}}
\hairline
\botrow{Contrarian}{contrarian} & Plays the move that beats your previous throw, after a random opening. & If you last played rock, expect paper and reply with scissors. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{You}{RPPSRSSP}}
\demo{\seq{Bot}{QPSSRPRR}\note{each throw beats the one above and to the left}}
\hairline
\botrow{Sticky}{sticky} & Repeats its last move 80\% of the time; each alternative has probability 10\%. & Counter its last move. Compare that with a bot that uses only total move counts. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{Bot}{RRRRPPPPPS}\note{a sample: long runs, then a jump}}
\hairline
\botrow{Switcheroo}{switcheroo} & Plays rock for the first half of the match and scissors afterwards. & Compare counts over the last 30 throws with counts over the whole match. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{Bot}{RRRRRR|SSSSSS}\note{switches at throw 250 of 501}}
\hairline
\botrow{Shifting Bias}{shifting\_bias} & Changes its favorite move every 50 throws, choosing it 70\% of the time. & Try a recent window or discount old counts. How long does your bot take to notice a switch? \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{Bot}{RRPRRRSRRR|PPPRPSPPPP}\note{a sample: throws 1--50, then 51--100}}
\hairline
\botrow{Win Stay Lose Shift}{win\_stay\_lose\_shift} & Repeats after a win or draw. After a loss, chooses either other move with equal probability. & After it wins or draws, counter its last move. After it loses, play the move its last move beats. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{You}{SRRPPSRR}}
\demo{\seq{Bot}{RRRRSSSP}\note{stays after a win or draw, shifts after a loss}}

\tablehead
\tierband{myorange}{Advanced}{Route 03 \textperiodcentered{} memory and context}
\botrow{Cycle Counter}{cycle\_counter} & Counters your most common move over the last ten throws. & Model its sliding window, including the tie-break from the earliest tied occurrence. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{You}{RPRRSPRSRP}\then\seq{Bot}{P}\note{rock led your last ten, so it plays paper}}
\hairline
\botrow{Double Take}{double\_take} & Repeats a nine-throw cycle. Each move and each adjacent pair appears equally often. & Record what follows each pair of moves. Compare this with a table using only the last move. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{Bot}{RRPRSPPSS|RRPRSPPSS}\note{starts at a random point in the cycle}}
\hairline
\botrow{Markov}{markov} & Learns what follows your last move and counters that prediction. & Reconstruct its counts from your own history, or balance what follows each of your moves. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{You}{RPRSRPR}\then\seq{Bot}{S}\note{after rock you have mostly played paper, so it plays scissors}}
\tierband{myred}{Expert}{Route 04 \textperiodcentered{} adaptation}
\botrow{Adaptive Ensemble}{adaptive\_ensemble} & Chooses among repeat, frequency, cycle and reactive predictors using their recent accuracy. & Change your rule partway through a match and measure how long it takes to catch up. \\[1pt]
\demo{\note{No fixed pattern to draw: after every throw it re-scores four predictors and follows the current leader.}}
\hairline
\botrow{Sequence Hunter}{sequence\_hunter} & Looks for earlier occurrences of your last 2--5 moves within your last 150 throws. & Try a sequence where the same five-move ending has different successors. Which does it predict? \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{You}{RPSRSPRPS}\then\seq{Bot}{P}\note{the last three throws appeared once before, followed by rock}}
\tierband{mygray}{Baseline}{No route \textperiodcentered{} the control opponent}
\botrow{Random Uniform}{random\_uniform} & Chooses each move independently with equal probability, using a seeded generator. & Use several seeds to see how much scores vary against random play. \\[1pt]
\demo{\seq{Bot}{PSRRSPSRPS}\note{a sample: a different seed gives a different sequence}}

{\large\bfseries Quick reference}\par\vspace{4pt}
\begingroup\small
\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{.56\linewidth}\raggedright
\textbf{A bot that beats Cycle RPS}\par\vspace{-3pt}
\begin{lstlisting}[style=python,numbers=none,xleftmargin=4pt,framexleftmargin=0pt]
COUNTER = {
    Move.ROCK: Move.PAPER,
    Move.PAPER: Move.SCISSORS,
    Move.SCISSORS: Move.ROCK,
}

def next_move(my_history, opponent_history, match_state):
    if not opponent_history:
        return Move.PAPER
    prediction = COUNTER[opponent_history[-1]]
    return COUNTER[prediction]
\end{lstlisting}
\code{COUNTER[move]} is the move that beats \code{move}: paper beats rock, rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper. Swap the prediction line to change which bot you beat.
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{.40\linewidth}\raggedright
\textbf{Playing a bot}\par\vspace{2pt}
\code{rps-cli play --against rocky}\par\vspace{2pt}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=1pt]
\item Longer match: \code{--best-of 501}
\item Several bots: \code{--against rocky,sticky}
\item Repeatable seeds: \code{--games 5 --seed 100}
\item Before submitting: \code{rps-cli test}, then \code{rps-cli validate}
\end{itemize}
\vspace{3pt}
\textbf{Reading history}\par\vspace{2pt}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \code{opponent\_history[-1]} is its last throw.
\item \code{my\_history[-2]} is your throw two turns ago.
\item Both are empty before the first throw: check \code{if not opponent\_history}.
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}
